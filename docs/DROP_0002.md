# DROP 0002 — Persona Playbooks

DROP 0002 turns every Chaos Pass profile from a scenario label into an explicit way to attack a program.

## Added

- Profile Schema v1.
- Universal Program Attack Model.
- Complete playbooks for all 15 profiles.
- Attack-surface priorities.
- Opening moves against an unfamiliar program.
- Controlled escalation ladders.
- Named signature tactics with signals to watch.
- Combination and handoff rules.
- Stop conditions.
- Required evidence.
- Clear separation between Random Chaos and adaptive Wildcard.
- Charlie's workaround-chain testing philosophy.
- Evidence-gated Final Boss behavior.
- CLI profile inspection.
- Tests that reject incomplete profile definitions.

A complete profile must answer:

> Who am I as a tester? What do I try first? What do I escalate? What signals make me pivot? What should I combine? When do I stop? What evidence do I bring back?
